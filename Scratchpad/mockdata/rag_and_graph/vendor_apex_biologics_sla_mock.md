# Master Service Agreement (MSA)
**Vendor:** Apex BioLogistics & Diagnostic Supplies Pvt. Ltd.
**Effective Date:** March 15, 2026

## 1. Services Provided
Apex BioLogistics agrees to supply RT-PCR molecular reagent packs, DNA extraction kits, and provide specialized refrigerated transport services to regional hubs.

## 2. Storage and Transportation Clauses
**Clause 2.1:** Primary warehousing will maintain the required continuous 2°C to 8°C environment.
**Clause 2.2.4:** For regional last-mile delivery routes under 48 hours, Vendor is permitted to utilize ambient passive insulated containers maintaining temperatures between 15°C to 25°C to reduce logistical overhead. 
**Clause 2.3:** Data loggers will be used for all primary routes, but are optional for last-mile routes under 48 hours.

## 3. Compliance and Certifications
The vendor operates a Schedule M certified facility and holds valid wholesale distribution licenses.

## 4. Liability Limit
**Clause 4.1:** Vendor's liability for any spoiled reagents or compromised logistics is strictly limited to 1.0x the invoice value of the affected batch. The buyer assumes all risk of subsequent clinical delays.
