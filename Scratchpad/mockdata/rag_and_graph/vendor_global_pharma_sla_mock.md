# Master Service Agreement (MSA)
**Vendor:** Global Pharma Logistics & Formulation Services Ltd.
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## 1. Scope of Services
Global Pharma Logistics agrees to manufacture, store, and transport bulk active pharmaceutical ingredients and biologic intermediates to the Buyer.

## 2. Storage and Transportation Guidelines
**Clause 2.1:** All biologic materials shall be stored at the vendor's primary facility in validated cold rooms maintained between 2°C to 8°C.
**Clause 2.2:** During overland transit, temperature will be maintained via insulated shipper boxes. Due to hardware limitations, continuous data logging is not provided; instead, manual temperature logging will occur at dispatch and arrival checkpoints.
**Clause 2.3:** A cumulative temperature excursion of up to 30 minutes reaching 10°C is permitted without triggering batch rejection, provided the arrival temperature is below 8°C.

## 3. Liability and Indemnification
The maximum aggregate liability of Global Pharma Logistics for any damaged goods, including cold-chain failures, shall be strictly capped at 1.5x the total purchase order value. Consequential damages are mutually waived.

## 4. Licensing
The Vendor warrants that they hold a valid Schedule M GMP certification and active Form 28-D manufacturing license issued by the CDSCO.
