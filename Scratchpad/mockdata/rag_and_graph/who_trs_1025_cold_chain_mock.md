# WHO Technical Report Series, No. 1025 (TRS 1025)
## Annex 7: Good Storage and Distribution Practices for Medical Products

### 1. Temperature and Environment Controls
All temperature-sensitive pharmaceutical and biological products MUST be transported under strictly controlled environmental conditions. 
For refrigerated biologic vaccines and reagents, the required temperature range is strictly **2°C to 8°C**.

### 2. Transportation and Transit Monitoring
**2.1 Data Logging:** 
Continuous temperature monitoring IoT sensors or data loggers must be active during the entire transit duration. 
Manual temperature logging at checkpoints is completely unacceptable and invalidates the batch integrity.

**2.2 Excursions:**
Any temperature excursion exceeding 8°C for more than 15 cumulative minutes during transit requires immediate batch quarantine and quality assurance review.

**2.3 Permitted Equipment:**
Only validated active refrigerated vehicles or certified passive cooling containers with phase-change materials (PCMs) proven to hold 2°C to 8°C for the maximum transit duration may be used.
