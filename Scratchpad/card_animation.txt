🎨 Key Elements Used in This Card:
Glassmorphism Layer: backdrop-blur-xl bg-card/80 border-border/80 rounded-3xl shadow-xl
Ambient Mesh Glow: Radial blurred primary light blobs behind the card (bg-primary/10 blur-3xl).
Tactile Button Physics: Rounded action buttons with spring hover lift (hover:-translate-y-0.5 hover:shadow-xl).
