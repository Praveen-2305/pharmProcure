## CHAPTER I
## INTRODUCTION

AutonoSource is a multi-agent procurement risk and vendor intelligence platform designed
to assist procurement analysts in heavily regulated pharmaceutical supply chains. The
platform integrates hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) using both a dense
vector retriever (Qdrant) and a NetworkX property graph to locate authoritative statutory
references, contract clauses, and vendor evidence.

The system coordinates specialized LangGraph AI agents — Planner, RAG, Scraper, Scorer,
Critic, and Writer — to perform a structured investigative workflow, deterministic pricing
checks in Indian Rupees (INR), contradiction detection, and human-in-the-loop approval.

Key features include: multimodal retrieval, cache-augmented generation, stateful revision
loops, and explainable report synthesis.
