# MASTER SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) & LOGISTICS SPECIFICATION

**DOCUMENT REFERENCE:** SLA-2026-APX-088  
**PARTIES:**  
- **Client:** State Central Medical Supplies & Hospital Procurement Board  
- **Service Provider / Vendor:** Apex BioLogistics & Diagnostic Supplies Pvt. Ltd.  
**EFFECTIVE DATE:** February 1, 2026  
**DOMAIN:** Cold-Chain Pharmaceutical Freight & In-Vitro Molecular Diagnostics Distribution  

---

### 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES & COMMODITIES
1.1 Provider agrees to deliver scheduled consignments of temperature-sensitive pharmaceutical agents, including:
- Recombinant Insulin Formulations (100 IU/ml vials)
- Viral Transport Media & RT-PCR Diagnostic Reagent Kits
- Amoxicillin & Clavulanate finished dosage oral suspensions

---

### 2. STORAGE, COLD-CHAIN & TRANSIT PROTOCOLS
2.1 **Primary Hub Storage:**  
All biological products shall be maintained in cold rooms calibrated at **2°C to 8°C** as set forth in WHO TRS 1025 guidelines.

2.2 **Secondary Transit Exemption Clause (CONTRADICTORY SPECIFICATION):**  
*Clause 2.2.4:* "For regional intra-state deliveries under 48 hours transit duration, ambient passive insulated containers maintaining up to **15°C to 25°C** are deemed acceptable and permitted where dedicated refrigerated reefers are logistically unavailable."  
*(Note: This clause is subject to high compliance audit risk under CDSCO Schedule M cold-chain mandates).*

2.3 **Temperature Logging:**  
Consignments shall include single-use chemical threshold indicators. Continuous digital data logging is provided only upon explicit prior written requisition at an additional surcharge of INR 1,500 per container.

---

### 3. STATUTORY PRICING & NPPA COMPLIANCE
3.1 Provider warrants that invoiced rates do not exceed statutory ceiling prices notified by the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) under DPCO 2013.  
3.2 Quoted base package deal value for annual hospital diagnostic kit replenishment: **INR 450,000.00**.

---

### 4. INDEMNITY, CURE PERIOD & LIABILITY CAPS
4.1 **Liability Cap:**  
Notwithstanding any provision herein, Provider's aggregate cumulative liability for direct damages, product spoilage, or shipment loss shall be strictly capped at **1.0x the invoiced purchase value** of the affected shipment.

4.2 **Indemnity Exclusions:**  
Provider disclaims all consequential, indirect, or regulatory penalty losses arising from batch quarantine orders issued by State Drug Controllers, unless direct criminal negligence is established.

4.3 **Default & Cure Period:**  
Client shall provide thirty (30) calendar days written notice of default to enable Provider to cure any operational deficiency.
